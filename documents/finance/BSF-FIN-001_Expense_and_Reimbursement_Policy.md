## Purpose

This policy establishes the rules for incurring, documenting, approving, reimbursing, recording, and reviewing reasonable business expenses incurred on behalf of BlueSea Foods. Its purpose is to ensure that corporate funds are used responsibly, consistently, transparently, and in support of legitimate business activities.

The policy protects employees and the Company by defining what expenses are reimbursable, what evidence is required, who may approve an expense, how exceptions are handled, and how potential misuse is investigated. It also supports accurate financial reporting, tax compliance, audit readiness, budget discipline, and the prevention of fraud, bribery, conflicts of interest, and duplicate claims.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to all BlueSea Foods directors, employees, temporary workers, trainees, and authorized contractors who incur approved expenses or use a Company-issued corporate card. It applies to expenses incurred for travel, customer and supplier meetings, site visits, training, business communications, authorized entertainment, and other approved business purposes.

It applies across corporate offices, processing plants, cold-storage facilities, sourcing offices, logistics operations, and all countries in which BlueSea Foods operates. Local procedures may define currency limits, preferred suppliers, tax receipts, payment platforms, and submission deadlines; however, they must comply with this policy.

This policy does not govern employee payroll, supplier invoices processed through procurement, capital expenditure approval, or petty cash administration, except where an employee seeks reimbursement for an approved out-of-pocket business payment. Expenses connected to product safety, cargo security, supplier integrity, or customer complaints must also comply with the relevant operational procedure.

## Definitions

**Business Expense:** A necessary, reasonable, properly authorized cost incurred exclusively to support BlueSea Foods business activities.

**Corporate Card:** A payment card issued by BlueSea Foods or its approved financial institution for authorized business expenditure. It remains Company property.

**Expense Claim:** A request submitted by an eligible claimant for repayment of personally paid, approved business expenses.

**Original Receipt:** The merchant invoice, fiscal receipt, electronic receipt, ticket, or equivalent record showing the supplier, date, amount, currency, and purchased goods or services.

**Per Diem:** A pre-approved daily allowance for eligible travel costs, applied according to the published local travel schedule.

**Approver:** A manager with delegated budget authority who verifies that an expense is legitimate, reasonable, supported, and within budget. An approver may not approve their own expense.

## Main Content

### 1. Core Principles

Employees must spend Company funds as carefully as they would spend their own funds while recognizing that BlueSea Foods requires a higher standard of evidence and accountability. Every expense must have a clear business purpose, be necessary for the activity performed, represent reasonable value, comply with applicable law and internal controls, and be supported by complete documentation.

Expense claims are not an employee benefit or a substitute for compensation. Reimbursement is limited to actual, approved business costs and may be refused where supporting evidence, business justification, approval, or compliance with this policy is missing. Finance may request clarification, reject a claim, deduct an ineligible item, or refer a concern to Compliance without delaying legitimate items unnecessarily.

Employees must choose cost-effective options consistent with operational needs, safety, and the importance of the business activity. Personal preference alone is not a justification for premium accommodation, transportation, meals, or other expenditure. Employees must not split a purchase, delay a claim, use another person’s card, or alter a description to avoid an approval threshold.

### 2. Pre-Approval and Budget Responsibility

The employee must obtain the appropriate approval before committing BlueSea Foods to material travel, hospitality, training, or any unusual expense. Approval should identify the purpose, estimated cost, cost center, participants where relevant, and expected dates. For routine local travel or low-value operational purchases, a standing approval may be established by the employee’s manager and Site Finance Manager.

The budget owner is accountable for confirming that planned expenditure is necessary and available within the approved budget. A manager’s approval confirms both the business purpose and budget availability; it does not remove Finance’s right to review documentation or Compliance’s right to review risk.

Expenses involving public officials, customs officers, inspection authorities, certification bodies, political organizations, charitable entities, or a third party acting on behalf of BlueSea Foods require prior written review by Compliance. No employee may use an expense claim to make a facilitation payment, improper gift, cash payment, or concealed benefit.

### 3. Reimbursable Expenses

Subject to approval and proper evidence, reimbursable expenses may include economy-class air or rail travel; reasonable hotel accommodation; local transportation; rental vehicles when commercially justified; fuel for an approved business journey; visas and required travel documents; business meals; required communication charges; approved training fees; and necessary materials purchased for a specific business activity.

Travel shall be booked through approved channels whenever available. Employees must select the lowest logical fare that meets business timing, safety, and operational requirements. Business-class travel is permitted only when expressly approved in advance by a Corporate Director or when a documented medical accommodation applies. Hotel selection must be safe, reasonably located, and aligned with the applicable city limit or preferred supplier program.

Meals may be reimbursed when an employee is traveling on authorized Company business, working outside the normal site for an approved purpose, or hosting a legitimate business meeting. The claim must identify the business purpose and attendees for hosted meals. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless authorized in advance for a permitted customer or stakeholder event and permitted by law and the Corporate Compliance and Ethics Policy.

For recurring travel, Finance may issue a per diem schedule. When a per diem is claimed, the claimant may not also claim individual meals or incidental items covered by that allowance. The employee must record travel dates, destination, business purpose, and any meals provided by a hotel, customer, event organizer, or airline.

### 4. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

BlueSea Foods will not reimburse personal expenses, including leisure travel extensions, minibar items, personal entertainment, family or companion costs, fines, parking violations, traffic penalties, lost personal property, passport replacement caused by personal negligence, personal grooming, clothing not required as approved protective equipment, or childcare.

The following are also non-reimbursable unless a written exception is approved before the expense: first-class travel; upgrades purchased for convenience; luxury accommodation; personal loyalty-program purchases; personal mobile-device purchases; cash advances not authorized by Finance; and expenses incurred while an employee is not performing approved business activity.

Gifts, donations, cash equivalents, gift cards, political contributions, and payments to public officials are prohibited unless specifically authorized under BSF-COMP-001 — Corporate Compliance and Ethics Policy and approved in writing by Compliance. A personal expense must never be charged to a corporate card, even if the employee intends to repay it later.

### 5. Evidence and Claim Submission

Employees must submit claims through the approved expense platform or form within 30 calendar days after the expense date or completion of travel. Claims submitted after 60 calendar days require written approval from the Site Finance Manager; claims older than 90 calendar days may be rejected unless an exceptional documented circumstance prevented timely submission.

Each claim must state the date, supplier, amount, currency, expense category, business purpose, cost center, and applicable project or customer code. Original receipts must be attached for all expenses unless a local legal threshold allows otherwise. A bank or card statement alone is not sufficient because it does not establish the business purpose or the goods and services purchased.

If an original receipt is unavailable, the employee must complete a missing-receipt declaration explaining why it is unavailable, the amount, supplier, date, business purpose, and confirmation that no duplicate reimbursement has been requested. The employee’s manager and Finance must approve the declaration. Repeated missing receipts may lead to refusal of reimbursement or further review.

Claims containing personal and business components must clearly identify the business portion. For example, if a trip is extended for personal reasons, only the documented incremental cost that would have been incurred for the approved business itinerary is eligible. The employee must obtain comparative booking evidence before travel whenever practical.

### 6. Corporate Card Controls

Corporate cards may be issued only to employees whose role requires regular business expenditure and whose manager, Finance, and Information Security approval are recorded. Cardholders must protect the card, personal identification number, and transaction credentials; promptly report loss, theft, suspected misuse, or unauthorized transactions; and return the card immediately upon role change, extended leave, or separation from employment.

Corporate cards are for approved business use only. Cardholders must reconcile all transactions within the monthly deadline established by Finance, attach supporting evidence, and identify the cost center and business purpose. Cash withdrawals are prohibited unless Finance grants prior written approval for a site-specific operational need.

The cardholder is personally responsible for charges that are unauthorized, unsupported, or personal. Finance may suspend a card for late reconciliation, repeated policy breaches, unresolved transactions, or suspected misuse. Serious or intentional misuse may result in repayment, disciplinary action, termination of employment, and referral to law enforcement where appropriate.

### 7. Approval Workflow and Segregation of Duties

The standard workflow is: claimant submission; line-manager review; budget-owner approval where different; Finance validation; payment or card reconciliation; and accounting record retention. Finance may return a claim for clarification at any stage. Claims must not be approved by the claimant, a direct report, or a person with a conflict of interest.

The approver must confirm that the expense is business-related, reasonable, within the budget, accurately coded, and supported by receipts. Finance must verify arithmetic, receipt completeness, policy compliance, tax treatment, duplicate claims, and required escalation. Higher approval is required when a claim exceeds the local authority threshold, includes hospitality, involves a public official, or represents an exception.

If an expense is urgent, the employee must document why pre-approval was not feasible and obtain retrospective approval as soon as possible. Urgency does not automatically make an otherwise prohibited expense reimbursable.

### 8. Compliance, Fraud Prevention, and Monitoring

Employees must not submit false, duplicate, inflated, altered, or misleading claims. They must not seek reimbursement from both BlueSea Foods and another organization, use expense funds to obtain an improper advantage, or request a supplier to misdescribe an invoice. Suspected fraud, bribery, conflict of interest, or retaliation must be reported through the channels in BSF-COMP-001 — Corporate Compliance and Ethics Policy.

Finance will conduct risk-based reviews that may include duplicate-receipt checks, unusual spend patterns, weekend or holiday transactions, repeated missing receipts, round-value claims, split transactions, claims outside the employee’s normal location, and comparisons with approved travel data. These reviews are preventive controls and do not presume wrongdoing.

Expense records contain limited personal and financial information. Finance and managers must process them only for legitimate business purposes, retain them securely, and restrict access to authorized personnel in accordance with BSF-LEGAL-001 — Data Privacy and Compliance Policy.

### 9. Exceptions and Disputes

Exceptions must be requested before the expense whenever possible. The request must explain the business need, cost, reason the standard rule cannot be followed, risk considerations, and proposed compensating control. The Finance Director approves corporate policy exceptions; Compliance approval is mandatory for gifts, hospitality, government interaction, conflicts, or integrity risk.

An employee who disputes a rejected claim may submit a written explanation and additional evidence to the Site Finance Manager within 15 calendar days. The Site Finance Manager will issue a documented decision or escalate the matter to the Finance Director. A dispute does not permit an employee to bypass the normal approval workflow.

## Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Employees and Cardholders | Obtain approval, choose reasonable options, submit complete and timely claims, protect corporate cards, and report suspected misuse. |
| Line Managers | Confirm business need, review evidence, verify reasonableness, and ensure employees understand this policy. |
| Budget Owners | Confirm budget availability and approve expenditure within delegated authority. |
| Site Finance Manager | Validate local implementation, review exceptions and late claims, supervise reimbursements, and maintain supporting records. |
| Finance Director | Own this policy, approve corporate exceptions, monitor control effectiveness, and report material issues to Corporate Management. |
| Compliance Officer | Review integrity-sensitive expenses, investigate reported concerns, and advise on anti-bribery and conflicts of interest. |
| Human Resources | Support disciplinary action when policy breaches are substantiated and maintain appropriate employee communications. |
| Internal Audit | Independently assess adherence to this policy and the effectiveness of related controls. |

## Records

The following records shall be retained in the approved financial system or controlled repository:

- Pre-approval requests and supporting travel or event documentation
- Expense claims, receipts, invoices, tickets, and missing-receipt declarations
- Corporate card issuance, reconciliation, suspension, and return records
- Approval and exception records
- Reimbursement payment records and accounting entries
- Compliance review, investigation, and corrective-action records where applicable
- Risk-based monitoring reports and audit findings

Records must be retained according to the applicable legal, tax, accounting, and document-retention requirements, and at minimum according to BSF-DOC-001 — Document Control Procedure.
