1. Purpose

This manual explains how authorized BlueSea Foods personnel use BlueTrack to record, review, approve, monitor and retrieve operational information related to seafood receiving, inspection, cold chain control, vessel authorization, traceability, corrective actions, and document evidence.

BlueTrack is designed to support daily operational discipline. It does not replace professional judgment, legal requirements, food safety procedures, customer specifications or certified management system controls. Instead, it provides a controlled digital workspace where users can execute required steps, attach evidence, generate alerts, and maintain traceable records for internal management and external audits.

Manual principle: If an operational event is not recorded in BlueTrack or in an approved contingency record, it may be treated as incomplete evidence during internal review, customer review or certification audit.

2. Scope

This manual applies to BlueSea Foods employees, supervisors, managers, approved contractors and temporary users who are granted access to BlueTrack. The system is used across the following process areas:

  • Raw material receiving and inspection.
  • Cold chain temperature monitoring and deviation follow-up.
  • Supplier and authorized fishing vessel verification.
  • Lot creation, batch identification and traceability linkage.
  • Quality hold, release, rejection and corrective action workflows.
  • Operational dashboards and compliance reporting.
  • Controlled access to approved procedures, catalogs, registers and evidence files.

BlueTrack is not the system of record for payroll, personal employee benefits, commercial accounting, customer invoicing or legal contract execution unless a specific approved integration is documented by Information Technology and the relevant process owner.

3. Confidentiality

This document is classified as Internal Use Only. It contains operational instructions, system process descriptions and examples of internal workflows. Users shall not share this manual, screenshots, system exports, vessel data, supplier information, customer references, lot records or quality findings outside BlueSea Foods unless such sharing is authorized by the document owner and aligned with the applicable confidentiality requirements.

Users must avoid posting BlueTrack screenshots in personal messaging applications, social networks, external AI tools, unmanaged cloud folders or unapproved collaboration spaces. When support evidence must be shared with IT or management, users shall follow the approved support channel and remove unnecessary personal, supplier or customer data where possible.

6. Access and Login

6.1 Access Request

  1. The employee's manager confirms the business need for BlueTrack access.
  2. The manager requests access through the approved IT service channel.
  3. The process owner confirms the role profile and required modules.
  4. IT creates or updates the user profile and confirms activation.
  5. The user completes required induction before entering production records.

6.2 Login

Step 1: Open the approved BlueTrack URL from a company-managed browser or device.
Step 2: Enter your corporate username and password. If single sign-on is enabled, use the corporate authentication prompt.
Step 3: Complete multi-factor authentication when requested.
Step 4: Confirm that your name, role and assigned facility are correct on the user menu.

6.3 Password and Session Rules

  • Do not store passwords in browsers on shared workstations.
  • Lock the screen before leaving a workstation unattended.
  • Log out at the end of the shift or when using a shared terminal.
  • Report suspicious login activity immediately to IT.

8. Receiving Module

The Receiving module is used to create the first controlled operational record for incoming seafood. The receiving record must be created as close as practical to the physical receipt of product and before any lot is moved to a controlled storage or production step.

8.1 Create a Receiving Record

  1. Select Operations > Receiving > New Receiving Record.
  2. Enter receiving date, facility, receiving bay, supplier, transport unit, product group and species.
  3. Select the authorized vessel or supplier source where applicable.
  4. Enter gross weight, tare weight, net weight, units, presentation and packaging type.
  5. Record arrival temperature and attach the supporting temperature evidence.
  6. Link required documents such as purchase order, transport document, certificate, landing declaration or supplier declaration.
  7. Save as draft if data is incomplete or submit for Quality inspection if all mandatory fields are complete.

8.2 Mandatory Receiving Fields

FieldRequirementCommon Error to Avoid
SupplierMust match an active supplier master record.Typing a similar commercial name instead of selecting the approved record.
SpeciesMust match the controlled species catalog.Selecting a general product family when a specific species is required.
VesselRequired when the product is vessel-linked or MSC/BASC traceability applies.Leaving vessel blank because the supplier name was entered.
TemperatureMust be entered with unit of measure and evidence.Uploading a photo but forgetting the numeric reading.
Lot IDGenerated by BlueTrack after required fields are saved.Creating manual lot codes outside the system.
Do not release product from receiving controls when supplier status, vessel authorization, temperature, required documents or identity verification are incomplete.

9. Cold Chain Module

The Cold Chain module records product and storage temperature information. It supports monitoring of receiving temperature, storage conditions, transport transfer, corrective actions and trend review.

9.1 Enter Temperature Reading

  1. Open the lot or storage location to be monitored.
  2. Select Add Temperature Reading.
  3. Enter date, time, reading value, unit, location, equipment ID and person responsible.
  4. Attach supporting evidence when required by procedure or customer specification.
  5. Select Save. BlueTrack will compare the value against the applicable limit.

9.2 Temperature Status

StatusMeaningRequired Action
Within LimitThe reading is within the approved range for the product/location.No deviation action required; continue monitoring.
WarningThe reading is close to the limit or missing supporting data.Review promptly and add comments or evidence if needed.
DeviationThe reading is outside the approved limit.Create or link corrective action, notify supervisor and Quality, and place product on hold if required.

Temperature deviations must be managed according to BSF-OPS-001 - Cold Chain Control Register and linked to quality disposition where product safety or customer requirements may be affected.

10. Quality Inspection Module

The Quality Inspection module is used to record sensory checks, physical condition, packaging condition, documentation conformity, temperature review and food safety observations. The inspection result determines whether a lot is released, rejected or placed on hold.

10.1 Complete an Inspection

  1. Open the submitted receiving record from the Quality queue.
  2. Confirm product identity, supplier, vessel, species, lot code and required documents.
  3. Record inspection parameters: appearance, odor, texture, packaging, labeling, foreign material risk, temperature and sample observations.
  4. Attach photographs or inspection forms where required.
  5. Select a disposition: Release, Hold, Reject or Escalate.
  6. If a nonconformity is detected, create a linked CAPA record before closing the inspection.

10.2 Disposition Rules

DispositionUse WhenSystem Impact
ReleaseRequirements are met and no blocking issue exists.Lot can proceed to the next authorized operation.
HoldInformation, evidence, inspection result or risk review is incomplete.Lot is blocked until authorized review is completed.
RejectProduct fails critical acceptance criteria or cannot be safely corrected.Lot is blocked and rejection workflow begins.
EscalateDecision requires manager, food safety team or customer input.Lot remains controlled pending higher-level decision.

11. Traceability and Vessel Verification

BlueTrack links product lots to approved suppliers, authorized vessels, species catalog records and relevant compliance evidence. This supports chain of custody, food safety traceability, supply chain security and audit readiness.

11.1 Verify an Authorized Vessel

  1. Open Supply Chain > Authorized Vessels.
  2. Search by vessel ID, vessel name, supplier, landing port or permit number.
  3. Confirm status: Authorized, Conditional, Suspended, Expired or Under Review.
  4. Review permit validity, owner/operator, applicable species and zone authorization.
  5. Confirm whether MSC Chain of Custody, BASC or customer-specific controls apply.
  6. Link the vessel record to the receiving lot or block the lot if the vessel is not valid for the intended operation.

11.2 Traceability Linkage

Each controlled lot should be traceable through the following relationship:

SpeciesSeafood species catalogScientific name, commercial name, risk class and temperature requirements.

15. Data Security and User Responsibilities

All BlueTrack users are responsible for protecting system data and maintaining the reliability of operational records.

15.1 User Responsibilities

  • Enter accurate, timely and complete information.
  • Use only the assigned personal account.
  • Attach clear and relevant evidence.
  • Report suspected errors, unauthorized access or system malfunction.
  • Follow document control rules when downloading or sharing information.
  • Respect confidentiality, privacy, customer and supplier requirements.

15.2 Administrator Responsibilities

  • Maintain user roles, configuration and master data controls.
  • Review access periodically with process owners.
  • Monitor system availability, backups and incident tickets.
  • Coordinate change management before releasing system updates.
  • Retain logs according to the approved retention schedule.

18. Records and Evidence

The following records may be generated, stored or referenced through BlueTrack:

  • Receiving records and lot creation logs.
  • Temperature readings and cold chain deviation actions.
  • Quality inspection results, product holds, releases and rejections.
  • Supplier and vessel status verification records.
  • Evidence attachments, including forms, photographs and certificates.
  • Corrective action and nonconformity records.
  • User access review and system activity logs.
  • Operational reports and audit evidence exports.

Records must be retained according to the applicable retention schedule and must not be deleted, overwritten or altered outside approved system workflows.
