## 1. Purpose

This document provides clear and direct answers to frequent questions that collaborators may ask through the BlueSea Foods Assistant.

It is designed to improve the quality of responses in the corporate RAG system by providing explanatory content, common user questions, Spanish and English search terms, keywords, and references to the most relevant source documents.

- food safety and HACCP;

This document does not replace official policies, procedures, manuals or regulatory requirements. When there is a conflict, the approved source document prevails.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions

### FAQ-001 - What is HACCP?

**Common questions:**

- What is HACCP?
- Que es HACCP?
- Explain HACCP.
- Para que sirve HACCP?

**Answer:**

HACCP means Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points. In Spanish, it is commonly translated as Analisis de Peligros y Puntos Criticos de Control.

HACCP is a preventive food safety system used to identify, evaluate and control hazards that may affect food safety. These hazards may be biological, chemical or physical.

At BlueSea Foods, HACCP supports the control of seafood processing, storage, freezing, dispatch and traceability activities. It helps ensure that products are handled under safe conditions and that critical points in the process are monitored before a food safety issue occurs.

**Responsible area:** Quality Assurance  
**Recommended source documents:** HACCP Plan, Food Safety and HACCP Management Manual, Food Safety Standards Guide  
**Keywords:** HACCP, food safety, inocuidad, critical control points, puntos criticos de control, hazard analysis, quality assurance

### FAQ-002 - What are freezing standards?

**Common questions:**

- What are the freezing standards?
- Cuales son las normativas para congelado?
- What rules apply to frozen seafood?
- Que normas aplican al congelado?

**Answer:**

Freezing standards are the internal, regulatory and customer requirements used to control the freezing, storage and handling of frozen seafood products.

At BlueSea Foods, freezing controls should cover product temperature, storage conditions, cold room monitoring, records, corrective actions and traceability evidence. These controls help prevent quality deterioration and food safety risks.

The specific temperature limits, inspection frequency and corrective actions must be validated in the approved Quality Assurance and Operations procedures.

**Responsible areas:** Quality Assurance and Operations  
**Recommended source documents:** Freezing and Storage Temperature Standards, Cold Chain Control Procedure, Food Safety and HACCP Management Manual  
**Keywords:** freezing, frozen seafood, congelado, temperature, temperatura, cold storage, cold chain, cadena de frio

### FAQ-003 - What is the cold chain?

**Common questions:**

- What is the cold chain?
- Que es la cadena de frio?
- Why is cold chain important?
- Para que sirve la cadena de frio?

**Answer:**

The cold chain is the set of controls used to keep seafood products under approved temperature conditions from receiving to storage, processing, dispatch and transport.

At BlueSea Foods, the cold chain helps protect product safety, product quality and customer requirements. If temperature conditions are not controlled, the product may be exposed to quality loss or food safety risks.

Cold chain activities include temperature readings, cold room verification, equipment monitoring, deviation reporting and corrective actions.

**Responsible areas:** Operations and Quality Assurance  
**Recommended source documents:** Cold Chain Control Procedure, BlueTrack User Manual, Food Safety and HACCP Management Manual  
**Keywords:** cold chain, cadena de frio, temperature control, freezing, storage, dispatch, transport

### FAQ-004 - What is the Cold Chain Module?

**Common questions:**

- What is the Cold Chain Module?
- Que es el modulo de cadena de frio?
- How does the Cold Chain Module work?
- Para que sirve Cold Chain Module?

**Answer:**

The Cold Chain Module is the BlueTrack section used to record, monitor and verify temperature-related activities.

It may be used to enter temperature readings, review temperature status, identify deviations and keep evidence of cold chain control. This supports operational traceability and food safety verification.

If a reading is outside the approved range, the responsible user must follow the applicable corrective action procedure and notify the relevant Operations or Quality Assurance contact.

**Responsible areas:** Information Technology, Operations and Quality Assurance  
**Recommended source documents:** BlueTrack User Manual, Cold Chain Control Procedure, Corporate Systems Access Guide  
**Keywords:** Cold Chain Module, BlueTrack, temperature readings, temperature status, modulo cadena de frio, trazabilidad

### FAQ-005 - What does Temperature Status mean?

**Common questions:**

- What is Temperature Status?
- Que significa Temperature Status?
- Explicame las temperaturas.
- What does a critical temperature mean?

**Answer:**

Temperature Status indicates whether a temperature record is within the expected control condition.

Common statuses may include:

| Status | Meaning | Required Action |
|---|---|---|
| Normal | The temperature is within the approved range. | Continue monitoring and keep the record. |
| Warning | The temperature is close to a limit or requires attention. | Review conditions and increase monitoring if required. |
| Critical | The temperature is outside the approved range. | Notify the responsible area and apply corrective action. |

The exact temperature limits and status rules must be confirmed in the approved Quality Assurance and Operations documents.

**Responsible areas:** Operations and Quality Assurance  
**Recommended source documents:** Freezing and Storage Temperature Standards, Cold Chain Control Procedure, BlueTrack User Manual  
**Keywords:** temperature status, temperatura, normal, warning, critical, corrective action, accion correctiva

### FAQ-006 - How do I access BlueTrack?

**Common questions:**

- How do I access BlueTrack?
- Como ingreso a BlueTrack?
- Como es el acceso y login?
- Access and Login?
- Login?

**Answer:**

Access to BlueTrack must be requested through the approved onboarding or access request process.

Before access is granted, HR or the responsible manager must confirm the user relationship with BlueSea Foods, the line manager must confirm the role requirements, and IT must create or update the user profile.

Once access is active, the user must sign in using the assigned corporate credentials and confirm that their name, role and assigned facility are correct.

Access must only be used by the authorized user. Shared accounts, credential sharing and unauthorized access are not allowed.

**Responsible area:** Information Technology  
**Recommended source documents:** Corporate Systems Access Guide, BlueTrack User Manual, Data Security and API Guidelines  
**Keywords:** access, login, BlueTrack, acceso, inicio de sesion, user profile, corporate credentials, IT

### FAQ-007 - What are Password and Session Rules?

**Common questions:**

- What are Password and Session Rules?
- Hablame sobre Password and Session Rules.
- What are password rules?
- Cuales son las reglas de contrasena?

**Answer:**

Password and Session Rules define how users must protect their corporate access credentials and how system sessions must be managed.

At BlueSea Foods, users must keep passwords confidential, avoid storing passwords in browsers on shared workstations, and report any suspected compromise. Passwords must not be shared with coworkers, contractors or third parties.

Sessions should be closed when work is finished, especially on shared or operational workstations. Unusual login activity, repeated failed attempts or suspected unauthorized access must be reported to IT.

**Responsible area:** Information Technology  
**Recommended source documents:** Corporate Systems Access Guide, BlueTrack User Manual, Data Security and API Guidelines  
**Keywords:** password, session, contrasena, sesion, credential sharing, login, account lockout, unauthorized access

### FAQ-010 - Why does the assistant say there is no information available?

**Common questions:**

- Why does the assistant not answer?
- Por que el asistente dice que no encontro informacion?
- Why does it say no evidence?
- Que significa no evidence?

**Answer:**

The assistant may say that it did not find enough information when the available documents do not contain clear evidence for the question.

This can happen when:

- the topic is not covered in the indexed documents;
- the document only mentions the term but does not explain it;
- the question is too general or ambiguous;
- the relevant document has not been reindexed after an update;
- the information is stored in a file type or section that is not easy to retrieve.

To improve the answer, ask a more specific question or update the source documents with definitions, procedures, examples and keywords.

**Responsible area:** Corporate Knowledge Management  
**Recommended source documents:** Corporate Glossary, Frequently Asked Questions, Corporate Document Standard  
**Keywords:** no evidence, low retrieval confidence, no informacion, asistente, RAG, reindex

### FAQ-013 - What should I do if I have a workplace concern?

**Common questions:**

- What should I do if I have a workplace concern?
- Que hago si tengo un problema laboral?
- Como reporto una queja laboral?
- A quien acudo por un problema en el trabajo?

**Answer:**

Workplace concerns should be reported through the appropriate internal channel. In most cases, the employee should first contact their direct supervisor, department manager or Human Resources.

The report should include clear facts, dates, people involved, available evidence and the impact of the situation. If the concern involves harassment, discrimination, retaliation, serious misconduct or a conflict with the direct supervisor, the employee may contact Human Resources or another authorized reporting channel directly.

BlueSea Foods must handle workplace concerns with confidentiality as far as reasonably possible, subject to the need to review, investigate and respond.

**Responsible area:** Human Resources  
**Recommended source documents:** Employee FAQ, Employee Onboarding Guide, Internal Communication Guidelines  
**Keywords:** workplace concern, grievance, complaint, queja laboral, reclamo laboral, Human Resources, HR, recursos humanos, report concern

### FAQ-018 - What is a conflict of interest?

**Common questions:**

- What is a conflict of interest?
- Que es un conflicto de interes?
- Can I work with a supplier I know?
- Debo declarar intereses personales?

**Answer:**

A conflict of interest exists when a personal, family, financial or external interest may improperly influence a business decision or job responsibility.

Employees must disclose possible conflicts of interest to their manager, Human Resources or Compliance before participating in decisions that could be affected. Disclosure does not always mean the activity is prohibited, but it allows the company to review the situation and define controls.

**Responsible areas:** Compliance and Human Resources  
**Recommended source documents:** Employee FAQ, Internal Communication Guidelines, Corporate Glossary  
**Keywords:** conflict of interest, conflicto de interes, supplier, proveedor, ethics, disclosure, declaracion, compliance
