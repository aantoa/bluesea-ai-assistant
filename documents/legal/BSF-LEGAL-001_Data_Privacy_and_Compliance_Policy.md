## Purpose

This policy defines minimum requirements for handling personal data, confidential information, compliance questions, legal requests, and integrity concerns at BlueSea Foods. It helps employees know what may be shared, what must be protected, and when Legal or Compliance must be involved.

This document is not legal advice and does not replace applicable law, regulatory instruction, customer contract, certification requirement, or specific direction from Legal or Compliance.

---

## Quick Answers for AI Retrieval

### What is personal data?

Personal data is information that identifies or can reasonably be linked to an individual. It may include employee records, identification numbers, contact details, payroll data, attendance records, medical restrictions, access logs, investigation records, training records, and supplier or customer contact details.

### How should personal data be handled?

Personal data must be collected, used, stored, shared, retained, and deleted only for approved business purposes and only by authorized personnel. Access must be limited to people with a legitimate need to know.

### Can employee information be shared?

Employee information may be shared only with authorized personnel for a legitimate business purpose. Sensitive records such as payroll, medical information, investigations, disciplinary records, identity documents, and access logs require stricter confidentiality.

### What is confidential information?

Confidential information is non-public business information that could harm BlueSea Foods, employees, customers, suppliers, or partners if disclosed without authorization. Examples include customer specifications, commercial terms, employee data, supplier performance, quality results, legal matters, security details, and internal investigations.

### Can company documents be sent outside the company?

Company documents may be sent outside BlueSea Foods only when the sender is authorized, the recipient has a legitimate need, the information classification permits it, and any required confidentiality, customer, privacy, legal, or contractual controls are followed.

### What should an employee do if an authority asks for information?

Requests from courts, regulators, inspectors, police, tax authorities, customs authorities, lawyers, auditors, or other official bodies must be escalated to Legal or Compliance before any document, statement, commitment, or data is provided.

### What is a legal hold?

A legal hold is an instruction to preserve records because of a legal, regulatory, audit, investigation, dispute, or authority matter. When a legal hold is issued, employees must not delete, alter, discard, or overwrite relevant information.

### What is a conflict of interest?

A conflict of interest exists when personal, financial, family, supplier, customer, or outside-business interests may affect or appear to affect an employee's judgment or duties. Potential conflicts must be disclosed before decisions are made.

### What should be reported to Compliance?

Employees should report suspected bribery, fraud, conflict of interest, retaliation, discrimination, harassment, data misuse, document falsification, unauthorized disclosure, improper gifts, or requests to bypass rules.

### Can the AI assistant answer legal advice questions?

The AI assistant may provide general policy routing based on approved documents, but it must not provide legal advice, interpret laws for a specific case, make legal decisions, or replace Legal or Compliance review.

### What should happen after a data incident?

Suspected data disclosure, incorrect recipient, lost device, exposed credential, unauthorized access, or privacy concern must be reported immediately to IT and Legal or Compliance. Employees must preserve evidence and avoid external communication unless instructed.

## Core Requirements

### 1. Lawful and Approved Purpose

BlueSea Foods information must be used only for an approved business purpose. Employees must not access records out of curiosity, personal interest, convenience, or pressure from an unauthorized person.

### 2. Need-to-Know Access

Access to personal data, confidential information, legal matters, investigations, payroll, and security information must be restricted to authorized personnel with a legitimate need to know.

### 3. Confidential Sharing

Before sharing information, employees must confirm the recipient, purpose, classification, channel, required approval, and any contractual or privacy restriction.

### 4. Authority and Legal Requests

No employee may provide records, admit liability, make commitments, respond to legal demands, or communicate with authorities on behalf of BlueSea Foods unless authorized by Legal or Compliance.

### 5. Investigation Integrity

Investigation information must be handled confidentially. Employees must not interfere with evidence, coach witnesses, retaliate, or discuss the matter outside authorized channels.

### 6. AI and RAG Restrictions

Restricted personal data, legal advice, investigation files, raw employee records, security-sensitive information, and obsolete controlled documents must not be added to a general AI or RAG repository unless the use case, access controls, and approvals have been formally validated.
