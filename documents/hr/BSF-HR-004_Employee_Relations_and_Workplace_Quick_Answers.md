## Quick Answers for AI Retrieval

### Who should answer workplace or labor questions?

Human Resources is the primary contact for workplace, employment, attendance, leave, benefits, payroll routing, employee records, grievances, resignation, and offboarding questions. Legal or Compliance must be involved when the matter includes legal risk, discrimination, retaliation, privacy, investigation, authorities, or potential policy breach.

### What should an employee do if they will be late?

The employee should notify the direct supervisor as soon as possible and follow the department attendance procedure. Repeated or unexplained lateness may require Human Resources review.

### What should an employee do if they cannot attend work?

The employee should notify the supervisor promptly, explain the reason at the level required by policy, and provide supporting documentation when required. Human Resources should be consulted for medical leave, protected leave, emergency leave, or repeated absence.

### How does an employee request leave or vacation?

The employee should submit the request through BlueHR or the approved leave process and obtain supervisor approval before making commitments. Leave approval depends on entitlement, staffing, operational needs, documentation, and applicable policy.

### What should an employee do if pay seems incorrect?

The employee should contact Payroll or Human Resources and provide the pay period, expected amount, actual amount, supporting records, schedule, overtime approval, or any relevant evidence. Finance or Payroll should review the calculation and communicate the resolution.

### Are benefits the same for all employees?

Benefits may vary by employment category, location, contract type, eligibility criteria, and applicable law. The controlled Leave and Benefits Policy and Human Resources records are the authoritative sources.

### How should workplace complaints be reported?

An employee may report a workplace concern to the direct supervisor, manager, Human Resources, Compliance, or another approved reporting channel. The report should include facts, dates, people involved, evidence, witnesses if any, and the impact of the concern.

### What is harassment or discrimination?

Harassment or discrimination involves conduct or decisions that treat a person unfairly or create an inappropriate workplace environment based on protected or prohibited grounds under applicable law or company policy. These concerns must be escalated to Human Resources and, when appropriate, Legal or Compliance.

### Is retaliation allowed?

No. Retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith, participates in a review, or reports suspected misconduct is prohibited. Suspected retaliation should be reported immediately to Human Resources or Compliance.

### What is a conflict of interest at work?

A conflict of interest exists when personal, financial, family, supplier, customer, or outside-business interests may improperly influence an employee's duties or decisions. The employee should disclose the potential conflict before acting.

### Can employees share company documents outside BlueSea Foods?

Employees may share company documents outside BlueSea Foods only when authorized, when the recipient has a legitimate business need, and when confidentiality, privacy, customer, legal, and document-control rules are followed.

### Can employee personal data be shared internally?

Employee personal data may be shared only with authorized personnel who have a legitimate business need. Payroll, medical, investigation, identification, and disciplinary data require stricter confidentiality.

### What happens during offboarding?

Offboarding may include final handover, return of company property, removal of system access, final payroll review, exit interview, confidentiality reminder, and record retention. Managers must coordinate with Human Resources and IT.

### Who handles legal or authority requests?

Requests from regulators, courts, authorities, auditors, lawyers, or external investigators must be escalated to Legal or Compliance. Employees should not provide documents, statements, data, or commitments unless authorized.
